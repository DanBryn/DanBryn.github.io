Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

Fun Wizard Uploader is a private tool operated by the owner of the Fun Wizard YouTube channel. It is not offered to the public.

What data the app uses

The app uses Google OAuth to access the channel owner's own YouTube account in order to upload videos, update video details and thumbnails, and read the channel's own YouTube Analytics.

How data is stored and shared

Access tokens are stored securely on the owner's own computer and are used only for the purposes above. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. The app does not collect data from any other users.

Google API Services

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
